Roadmap to the platinum

Three stages, about 200 hours in all. Stage 1 is the story; stage 2 is the 100% completion checklist; stage 3 is the stranger strands and the last cleanups. Nothing on the checklist is missable — only the Chapter 1 meditation in stage 1 can be lost.

The 100% completion checklist (A Long Road)

Unlocked after the story under Progress > Stats > 100% Completion. It is lenient — roughly 60% true completion — and progress made during the story already counts.

Missables & the watch list

There is 1 possibly missable trophy and 7 questlines with chapter windows. Everything else stays available after the story in free-roam. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter — there is no mission replay and 15 manual save slots.
